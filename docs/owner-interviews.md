# Owner interview guide (20 minutes each, 3–5 owners)

Goal: confirm or change the jobs in the PRD before building past Day 3. Ask
about past behaviour, not opinions about your idea. Don't pitch until the end.

## Who
Independent restaurant or cafe owners/managers with 300+ Google reviews.
Ask friends and family for intros; walk into 2–3 local places at a quiet hour.

## Questions
1. Tell me about your restaurant: how long, how many staff, dine-in vs delivery?
2. How often do you look at your Google reviews? Walk me through the last time.
3. **Tell me about the last time your rating dropped. What did you do?**
   (How did you find out? How did you figure out why? What did you change?)
4. How do you decide what to fix first when several things are going wrong?
5. Did you ever make a change because of reviews? How did you know if it worked?
6. Do you reply to reviews? Who does it, and how long does it take?
7. How do you share customer feedback with your kitchen and floor staff?
8. Do you ever compare yourself with places nearby? How?
9. Have you used any tool for this? What did you like or hate about it?
10. (Now show the dashboard or a sketch.) What would you do differently after
    seeing this? What's confusing? What's missing?

## After each interview, write down
- Which jobs (J1–J11) came up unprompted?
- Anything that surprised you or contradicts the PRD?
- Exact quotes worth using in your portfolio (ask permission).
